### Selected Projects

#### 🎓 [AI Career Development Platform](https://github.com/sriram162007/AI-Powered-Career-Development-Platform)
A full-stack career platform designed to help students analyze their career readiness and prepare for technical jobs.
- **Resume Analysis & ATS Scoring:** Parses uploaded resumes (`.pdf`, `.docx`) and calculates ATS match compatibility.
- **AI Mentorship & Mock Interviews:** Integrates Google Gemini API to offer specific bullet-point feedback and role-tailored interview practice.
- **Tech:** React 19, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, Firebase Auth & Firestore, Tailwind CSS

#### 🏢 MANAI CRM
A production-focused CRM system designed for real estate businesses to streamline day-to-day operations.
- **Core Modules:** Lead assignment pipelines, team role management (RBAC), property cataloging, and omnichannel client communications.
- **Status:** Core architecture built; in active feature development.
- **Tech:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Firebase Firestore, Framer Motion

#### 💬 WhatsApp AI Expense Tracker
An automated personal finance bot built to log expenses through conversational messaging.
- **Multimodal Ingestion:** Captures expenses via voice messages, text, and photos of paper receipts using OCR.
- **Workflow Automation:** Uses **n8n** and **Twilio WhatsApp API** to route messages, extract amounts and categories via Gemini AI, and append records directly to Google Sheets.
- **Tech:** n8n, Twilio WhatsApp API, Gemini AI, Google Cloud Sheets

#### 🎫 [QR Resolution System](https://github.com/sriram162007/Qr-resolution)
A digital ticketing and grievance tracking platform based on physical QR codes.
- **Friction-Free Reporting:** Users scan location-tagged QR codes to submit issue tickets without creating an account.
- **Real-Time Lifecycle:** Live status tracking page, admin resolution dashboard, and automated reporter alerts via WhatsApp.
- **Tech:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Firebase, Twilio API
